How to read this board. Implied value = TVL × a fitted value multiple × fitted launch odds. The multiple is a regression of ln(market cap / TVL) on the 87 traded peers with a market cap of at least $5M; the odds are a logistic fit on the 645 dated protocols (327 record a token by the snapshot date). A protocol is listed as recording no token, never as having none. Farm economics assume a launch gives 5% of implied value to farmers and competing farm capital of 8% of TVL; verdicts: FARM when expected net ≥ $75 and ≥ $25/h, MARGINAL when net > 0, else SKIP.
